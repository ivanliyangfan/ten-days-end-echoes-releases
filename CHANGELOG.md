# 0.2.23 — Drowned morph trident (Alpha)

Press V in Drowned form to throw a vanilla trident along your aim, with a 3-second cooldown. No inventory trident is consumed; spawned tridents cannot be picked up, preventing item farming. Silence and Echo seals prevent activation. Updated bilingual hints and starter handbook.

Regression coverage includes projectile type, owner, actual collision damage, caster attribution, pickup restriction, cooldown and cleanup, together with the existing morph and handbook suites.

This remains an alpha. Full species parity and actual two-client acceptance are unfinished; back up worlds before updating. Use matching mod versions on clients and servers.
